What Is PieceNote?

PieceNote is an app where you create pages inside notes and organize your information by writing memos on those pages. It is designed for gradually collecting short memos and organizing them later by moving, copying, reordering, and exporting them.

Rather than forcing you to sort everything perfectly from the beginning, PieceNote is designed around a simple flow: write first, organize later.

Basic Structure

PieceNote organizes information in three levels.

Notes

These are the main categories. You can use them for work, study, hobbies, journaling, and other broad themes.

Pages

Pages are smaller categories inside a note. They are useful when you want to divide a single note by topic, item, or purpose.

Memos

Memos are the actual pieces of content you write. You can use them for ideas, records, drafts, reminders, and more.

For example, you could create a note called “Reading Notes,” then add pages such as “Books to Read,” “Finished Books,” and “Favorite Quotes,” and write memos inside each page.

Understanding the Screen

Pages Tab

In the Pages tab at the top left, you can view the list of pages in the current note. Use this tab when you want to add a page or switch to another page.

Long-press a page to select it. Then you can use the action panel at the bottom of the screen to perform page actions, including exporting that page.

Memos Tab

In the Memos tab, you can view the list of memos on the selected page. Use this tab to read, create, and edit memos.

Long-press a memo to select it. Then you can use the action panel at the bottom of the screen to move, copy, merge, delete, and manage selected memos.

Bottom Action Panel

The bottom action panel shows actions such as Add and Reorder in the Pages tab and Memos tab. When an item is selected, it switches to actions for the selected page or memo.

Search Icon

Use the search icon at the top right of the home screen to open the memo search screen. It is useful when you want to quickly find a specific memo.

Drawer

Open the Drawer from the menu in the top left to switch notes. You can also open Inbox and Recent Memos there.

Long-press a note to select it. Then you can use note actions, including exporting that note.

Getting Started

1. Create a Note

Start by creating a note that matches the theme you want to manage. If you want to separate large categories of information, creating different notes will make things easier to organize.

2. Create Pages

Next, create pages inside the note. Pages help you divide your content further. You can separate them by topic, date, purpose, or any structure that works for you.

3. Write Memos

Open the Memos tab and start writing memos on the page. Since memos can be moved or copied later, you do not need to worry too much about perfect organization at the start.

Editing Memos

Tap a memo to edit its contents. This makes it easy to add more details or make quick corrections later.

Because each memo is independent, PieceNote works well not only for writing long text in one place, but also for managing many short memos separately.

Collapsing Memos

Tap the arrow icon in the top-right corner of a memo to collapse or expand its content.

When collapsed, only the first line of the memo is shown, wrapping to a maximum of two lines. This is useful for organizing long memos or memos whose content you usually want to keep hidden.

The collapsed or expanded state is saved and remains the same even after you close and reopen the app.

Organizing Memos

Select with a Long Press

Long-press a memo to select it. Once selected, an action panel will appear at the bottom of the screen.

Move

You can move selected memos to another page or another note. This is useful when you want to reorganize your content later or when you wrote something in the wrong place.

Copy

You can copy selected memos to another page or another note. This is useful when you want to use the same content in more than one place. The original memo remains where it is.

Multiple Selection

While one memo is selected, tap other memos to select multiple memos. This is helpful when you want to perform actions on several memos at once.

Merge

If you select two or more memos and choose Merge, they can be combined into a single memo. This is useful when you want to turn several smaller memos into one organized memo later.

Page and Note Actions

Pages and notes can also be selected with a long press. After selecting them, you can use the action panel to rename, delete, export, and perform other available actions.

Reordering Items

You can change the order of notes, pages, and memos by switching to reorder mode from the Reorder button.

  • Change the order of notes
  • Change the order of pages
  • Change the order of memos

The Reorder button for pages and memos appears in the bottom action panel on the home screen. Note reordering is available from the Drawer.

This makes it easy to rearrange your content into a more readable order after you write it. It works especially well with the “write first, organize later” style.

Exporting Memos

Export lets you take your PieceNote memos out as files. You can keep the exported files for safekeeping, open them on your computer or in other apps, or share them with others.

Export Range

  • Export a single page
  • Export one notebook
  • Export all notebooks

Export a Single Page

To export a single page, go to the Pages tab on the home screen, then long-press the page and choose Export from the action panel.

Export One Notebook or All Notebooks

To export one notebook or all notebooks, open the Export screen from Settings. You can also export one notebook by long-pressing it in the Drawer.

TXT and Markdown

TXT exports the text content without formatting or rich text styles. It is useful when you want to keep a simple plain text file.

Markdown exports headings, lists, checkboxes, and other supported formatting in Markdown. Some styles, such as underline and text color, are not preserved.

Export is different from Backup. If you want to keep your app data in a form that can be restored later, use the Backup feature in Settings.

Using Inbox

Inbox is a place to keep memos that you have not sorted yet. It is separate from your regular notes and can be opened from the Drawer.

You can use Inbox in situations like these:

  • Quickly write down an idea before you forget it
  • Decide later which note it belongs to
  • Keep temporary memos in one place

Inbox is useful when you do not want to stop and think about where something should go. You can place memos there first and move them to the appropriate note or page later.

Searching Memos

Search helps you find the memo you want by searching its content. It becomes especially useful as your memos grow over time.

Open the Search Screen

You can open Search from the search icon at the top right of the home screen. Enter a word or phrase, run the search, and matching memos will appear in a list.

Open a Memo from Search Results

Tap a result to jump directly to the page that contains that memo. This makes it easy to find what you need without manually checking each page.

Highlighted Matches

While searching, matching words are highlighted. This makes it easier to spot where your search term appears, both in the result list and in the memo you open.

If you remember part of what you wrote but not where you wrote it, Search is usually the fastest way to find it.

Viewing Recent Memos

Recent Memos shows your recently updated memos in order from newest to oldest. You can open it from the Drawer.

Reopen What You Worked on Recently

If you want to continue editing a memo you touched a little while ago, this view helps you find it quickly without tracing back through notes and pages.

See Which Note and Page It Belongs To

Each item also shows its note name and page name, so you can understand where the memo is located while browsing the list.

Jump Directly from the List

Tap any item to jump to the page that contains that memo. It is useful when you want to resume work or review your recent activity.

About Trash

Deleted memos are not removed immediately. Instead, they are moved to Trash. If you delete something by mistake, you can restore it from there.

Memos in Trash are deleted after 30 days. If you want to restore a deleted memo, be sure to check Trash before then.

Tips for Using PieceNote

Do Not Split Pages Too Much at First

If you create too many pages from the beginning, it may become harder to decide where to write. It is usually better to start simple and add more pages only when needed.

Use Inbox When You Are Unsure

If you are not sure where something belongs, put it in Inbox first. That way, you can keep writing without interrupting your flow and sort it out later.

Use Search as Your Shortcut

Once you have many memos, searching can be faster than opening pages one by one. It is also helpful when you want to look back at something you wrote earlier.

Resume Work from Recent Memos

If you want to reopen something you edited recently, Recent Memos is a convenient place to start. It works well for picking up where you left off.

Keep Memos Short

Shorter memos are easier to move, copy, and merge. If you want your notes to stay flexible, writing in smaller pieces usually works better.

Reorder Later

The order in which you write things is not always the best order for reading them later. Feel free to write naturally first, then reorder everything afterward.

Export Important Memos

If you want to keep important memos outside the app or use them in another app, export them as TXT or Markdown files.

Summary

PieceNote is an app that organizes information in the form of Notes → Pages → Memos.

  • Use the Pages tab to view pages
  • Use the Memos tab to write memos
  • Use the Drawer to switch notes
  • Use Inbox as a temporary place for unsorted memos
  • Use Search to find the memo you need
  • Use Recent Memos to reopen recently updated content
  • Organize everything later by moving, copying, and reordering
  • Export memos as TXT or Markdown files

Once you get used to this flow, it becomes easier to keep writing and organizing without stress.
