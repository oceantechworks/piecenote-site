PieceNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2025

OceanTechWorks ("the Developer") establishes this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of user data in the smartphone application "PieceNote" ("the App").

Article 1 (Information Collected)

  1. Memo content, page information, and other data entered or saved by users are stored locally on the device. Such data is not transmitted to the Developer’s servers.
  2. To improve usability, the App may use the following third-party services:
    • Google Analytics (usage analysis)
    • Google AdMob (advertising delivery)
  3. These services may automatically collect information such as advertising identifiers, operating system information, device model information, and usage events. This information does not include data that personally identifies users.

Article 2 (Data Destinations and Management)

Data collected by the third-party services listed above is transmitted to and stored on servers operated by Google LLC. Please refer to the following policies for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies

Article 3 (Purpose of Use)

  1. Improving app quality (performance analysis, improvements, and issue resolution)
  2. Providing and optimizing advertisements
  3. Analyzing usage to enhance user experience

Article 4 (User Choices)

  1. Users may reset their advertising ID or disable personalized ads through their device settings.
  2. Depending on the region, a consent management dialog may be displayed when required.

Article 5 (Data Storage and Deletion)

  1. All data created within the App is stored only on the user’s device. Uninstalling the App will delete all such data.
  2. Retention periods for data sent to third-party services are governed by the respective service providers’ policies.

Article 6 (Provision to Third Parties)

The Developer will not provide personal information to third parties without user consent, except as required by applicable laws and regulations.

Article 7 (Changes to This Policy)

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. Any changes will take effect upon being posted on this page.
