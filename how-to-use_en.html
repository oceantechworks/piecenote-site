PieceNote - How to Use

An overview of the Notes / Pages / Memos structure and how to use each feature.

💡 What is PieceNote?

PieceNote is an app for collecting small pieces of ideas and shaping them into meaningful notes.

You don’t need to decide on a structure right away. Start by writing freely in the MemoPool, and organize everything later—

or begin by writing directly on a specific page of a specific note.

Both styles are fully supported.

🏗️ Basic Structure

PieceNote is built on three simple layers:

Notes  >  Pages  >  Memos

Think of a note as a notebook, pages as sections inside it, and memos as individual pieces of content placed freely on each page.

And the MemoPool —

PieceNote includes a special space called the MemoPool, which does not belong to any specific note.

It acts as a shared hub that always appears on the far left. Memos in the MemoPool can be moved freely to any note or page.

Managing Notes (Drawer)

Tap the three-line icon in the top-left corner to open the drawer.

You can do the following here:

  • Create a new note (+)
  • Drag the handle on the right to reorder notes
  • Long-press a note to select one or more:
    • Edit icon → Edit
    • Trash icon → Delete

Managing Pages (Tab Bar)

  • When a note is open, pages appear in the tab bar at the top.
  • Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right to Create / Edit / Reorder / Delete pages
    (Tap the menu when no memos are selected)

Managing Memos

On pages or in the MemoPool, you can perform the following actions on memos:

  • Tap the round button in the bottom-right to add a new memo
  • Tap a memo to edit its content
  • Drag the handle on the right to reorder memos (no long-press required)
  • Long-press a memo to select one or more
    From the three-dot menu while memos are selected:
    • Move — move memos to another page
    • Merge — combine multiple memos into one
    • Delete — deleted memos are moved to the trash
